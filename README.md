## Selected Quantified Impact

| Project | Problem Solved | Quantified Impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **AURA HealthOS** | Hospital operations and life-critical utilities lack integrated closed-loop safety containment. | Connected 22 clinical departments, built 6-stage closed-loop workflows with bounded automation, and delivered responsive Three.js 3D campus. |
| **AstroBone Twin** | Astronauts face microgravity-induced bone loss and lack explainable in-flight fracture decision support. | Coupled Level-A Simulink control envelope with DenseNet-121 classifier and UNet++ segmentation; deterministic client verification rejects baseline mismatches. |
| **Multimodal ED Deterioration** | Emergency departments need early, calibrated warning of acute deterioration within the first 90 minutes. | Modeled 15 clinical targets on MIMIC-IV-Ext-MDS-ED using Gated ResNet/Transformers; validated with AUROC, AUPRC, Brier score, ECE, and decision curves. |
| **NeuroBridge SenseAssist** | At-home neuro-rehab lacks adaptive multi-sensory cueing and hard clinical safety bounds. | Coordinated 7-stage multi-agent pipeline with PulseSight rPPG; passed 10/10 reproducible software regression scenarios and 80/80 safety assertions. |
| **SMN2 Resilience Analysis** | Target discovery in SMA requires separating true biological resilience from donor heterogeneity. | Re-quantified 18 raw FASTQs (rho 0.778 concordance); confirmed 4 robust candidate genes (LY6H, HS3ST5, ZNF853, IL17D, p=0.044) and STAR-rMATS splice junctions. |
| **PDAC Biomarker Discovery** | Early pancreatic cancer detection needs reliable, non-invasive biomarker candidates. | Integrated 5 datasets, narrowed 628 malignant-switch genes to 80 spatially validated genes, and built a 6 EV-miRNA panel with ROC AUC 0.935. |
| **Cas12aDetect-R** | Diagnostic CRISPR-Cas12a detection of cancer SNVs needs optimized guide discrimination against wild-type. | Curated pan-cancer actionable SNV atlas with GRCh38 coordinates and scored candidate crRNAs across mismatch, structure, and off-target features. |
| **Microscopy Image AI** | Fluorescence microscopy drug screens need consistent normalization and per-cell quantification. | Compared 5 normalization strategies and trained U-Net segmentation with Dice ~0.90, IoU ~0.82, precision ~0.91, and recall ~0.90. |
| **NeuroRoboTwin** | Soft robotic hands need safer reflex behavior before physical actuator testing. | Modeled 4 reflex-control modes, targeted 20-50 ms biological latency, and reduced high-load inhibitory gain from 1.00 to ~0.78. |
| **ECG Analytics** | Raw ECG recordings need interpretable features for physiological-state comparison. | Converted recordings into 5 outputs: R-peaks, RR intervals, HRV, SDNN/RMSSD, and continuous wavelet maps. |
| **Gait Monitoring System** | Gait rehabilitation systems need closed-loop pneumatic and sensory corrective feedback. | Integrated 4 feedback streams (motion, plantar pressure, pneumatic support, vibration) with PWM-driven actuation. |
| **Drug Delivery Optimization** | Nanoparticle carrier selection needs statistically defensible comparison. | Used 3x3 DOE, ANOVA, and Tukey validation; polymer delivery averaged +9.2 percentage points over Au-based delivery. |
